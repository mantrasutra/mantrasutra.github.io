What this site claims

  • C1The site runs the same Python engine the project tests against, inside the browser via Pyodide. There is no second implementation (DESIGN 9c).
  • C2Values are computed with mpmath, up to 30 digits shown here. Sample grids are byte-identical across venues (T-V).
  • C3The सूत्र to function map is an isomorphism with a countable term algebra, up to the canonical form प्रकृतिः. It is NOT a claim about all analytic functions.
  • C4Refusals are part of the product: unsupported or malformed input returns a named refusal, not a guess.
  • C5Only layers L1 text, L2 prosody and L6 structure are available. L3, L4 and L5 are not in this version.
  • C6L6 figures carry their engine status (verified or experimental). Experimental ones are shown as experimental.
  • C7The vocabulary is attested words only. The coined words are shelved; 53 of the 67 rows in words.tsv are TBD (counted at build time) and the release check refuses TBD. The word list is incomplete by design.
  • C8B3 FAILED. In the single run, part (a) numerical scored 34/53 = 64.2% against a bar of 95% (B3-result.md in the sutra repo). The round trip was exact on 49/49 mapped rows.
  • C9Domain pack formulas are standard textbook forms evaluated by the engine. The physical commentary is illustrative and not engine-verified. Pack titles are proposals pending the owner's naming ruling.
  • C10Example mantra and verse texts are spec-supplied inputs, not a corpus claim.
  • C11Known limitation: a gamma pole in value reports Unsupported (0x3A7) rather than a numeric-domain code.
  • C12First load downloads about 25 MB (engine). No other network request is made.
  • C13Nothing here is a benchmark claim. The DESIGN section 0 non-promises apply: the map is between strings and a countable term algebra, not all analytic functions; exactness is syntactic, so sin²x + cos²x and 1 are different terms; semantic equivalence beyond the canonical rewriting is reported by numerical checks, never claimed. (Quoted from DESIGN 0; builder to re-check against the repo file.)

Limitations

Layers L3 to L5 are absent. The word list is incomplete. A gamma pole is reported as Unsupported. Names in Devanagari are placeholders where not yet ruled.
