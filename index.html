सूत्रम्

Sūtra: classical Sanskrit mathematical rules, read as typed terms and evaluated with certified precision.

What it is

Sūtra is an engine and a set of browser viewers. It takes a rule written as a sūtra document, parses it into a canonical term, and evaluates the term to a requested number of digits, reporting the digits it can certify. It also converts a term back to the sūtra text, and to LaTeX and an English gloss. The viewers plot a function and its sūtra side by side and edit formulas.

A second line of work analyses Sanskrit verse (word segmentation, metre, accent, number-words). It exists as a design and as registered tests with their recorded results; see below for its status.

What was measured

Each figure below comes from a result file in the repository. Every test was registered before it was run, and failures are reported.

TestResultSource file
N1: numerical accuracy of the engine against an mpmath reference, 176 points (30-digit value, reported error bound, binary64 sample path)176/176 on each pathdocs/N1-result.json, docs/N1-registration.md
B3 (a): reproduce every printed number of 53 worked examples from classical texts (e.g. the Līlāvatī)34/53 (64.2%). FAIL against the registered 95% bardocs/B3-result.md
B3: round trip sūtra text, parse, emit, equal to the text, on the 49 mapped rows49/49docs/B3-result.md
B3 (b): fidelity of the rule, scored by two LLM raters against a rubric, 39 items38/41 reported by the file; FAIL against the 95% bardocs/B3-result.md

Limits. The N1 reference is mpmath, and the engine itself evaluates with a vendored mpmath 1.3.0, so N1 tests the engine's term handling and error bounds, not mpmath. The N1 result was recorded before later engine fixes; it has not been re-run since. B3 failed overall: four examples need a data-dependent loop the engine cannot express, and nine failures come from numbers the blind mapping left out.
